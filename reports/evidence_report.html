What the evidence says

  1. 732/737 zero-student institutions are churn-labelled (99.32%), versus 16/50 with 51+ students (32.00%). This association does not establish cause.
  2. 486 institutions have zero values across all seven indicators. Two entire activity fields contain only zeros.
  3. 19 active-labelled institutions have no teacher or no class recorded. Validate their current status and applicable workflow before outreach.

Recommended next actions

  1. Validate population and tracking. Resolve zero-student eligibility, constant activity fields and engagement-score semantics.
  2. Pilot guided setup. After fresh eligibility checks, test support for accounts lacking teachers/classes.
  3. Test material-workflow assistance. Use an institution-randomised experiment and measure 30-day qualifying activity, plus support burden.
